Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly ("Spendly", "the Service", "we", "us") collects when you use our personal expense tracking application, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep the Service simple, and we aim to keep the data we hold about you just as minimal.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the Service for you:

  • Account information — the name, email address, and password you provide when registering. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Expense data — the records you choose to enter, such as amount, category, date, and description.
  • Technical information — basic details generated automatically when you use the Service, such as session data and error logs, used to keep the application working correctly.

We do not ask for and do not want your bank credentials, card numbers, or any other payment details. Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • Store, display, and organise the expenses you record.
  • Generate the summaries, category breakdowns, and filters shown in the app.
  • Diagnose faults, prevent abuse, and improve the reliability of the Service.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database and is accessible only through your own authenticated account. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, the associated expense records are removed from the live Service; residual copies may persist briefly in routine backups before being overwritten.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your own copies of any records that matter to you.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of external providers to operate — for example, hosting and infrastructure providers, and web font delivery for the site's typography. These providers may process limited technical data, such as your IP address, purely in order to deliver their part of the Service.

We do not grant third parties access to your expense records for their own purposes. Any provider we use is expected to handle data in line with applicable data protection requirements. Links from Spendly to external websites are outside our control, and their own privacy policies apply once you leave the Service.

5. Contact Us

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. You can also close your account, which removes your records from the live Service.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how your data is handled, please get in touch at privacy@spendly.example and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.
